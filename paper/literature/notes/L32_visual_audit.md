# Visual figure audit: L32

Source: `pdfs/L32.pdf`; viewed rendered pages 2 and 4 (journal pagination).

- Figure 1 (p. 2): mutation and molecular-feature landscape across 348 tumors: luminal A n=225, luminal B n=126, HER2-enriched n=57, basal-like n=93. The plotted rows/columns encode genomic and clinical features; the bottom percentage strip is printed in the figure. Its dense individual gene percentages should be transcribed from the full-resolution page only when needed for a specific claim.
- Figure 2 (p. 4): integrated subtype analysis across five genomic/proteomic platforms; consensus clusters are shown with n=348. No single summary performance metric is plotted.
- Caption/text values visible on inspected pages include PIK3CA mutation frequencies of 45% in luminal A, 25% luminal B, 10% HER2-enriched, and 6% basal-like; TP53 frequencies of 12%, 29%, 80%, and 80%, respectively. These are subgroup descriptive frequencies, not predictive performance.

Figures are dense descriptive cohort graphics; do not treat visual cluster separation as an externally validated prediction result.
