# Visual figure audit: L36

Source: `pdfs/L36.pdf`; viewed rendered pages 1-2 (journal pagination).

- Figure 1 (p. 1): mutation landscape for 230 lung adenocarcinoma resections, with statistical comparison data drawn from 412 TCGA cases. Caption identifies panels for co-mutation plot, mutation-type landscape, and sex-associated mutation patterns. The figure is descriptive; no prediction performance metric.
- Figure 2 (p. 2): aberrant RNA transcripts associated with DNA rearrangement or mutation. Caption reports 22 samples with insufficient coverage around exon 14 for MET splice quantification; among quantifiable cases the figure compares MET exon-14 skipping by mutation group. The plotted bars are proportions, with no single cohort-level prediction metric.
- Abstract/text visible on p. 1 reports a mean of 8.9 somatic mutations per megabase in the resections and 18 significantly mutated genes. The text on p. 2 reports 412 tumor/normal pairs for the TCGA comparison; KRAS mutations 33%, EGFR 14%, BRAF 10%, PIK3CA 7%, MET 7%, and STK11 17%.

These are molecular-provenance findings, not comparable survival-classification scores.
