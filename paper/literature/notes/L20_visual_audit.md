## Figure 4, visual-only leaderboard, PDF page 7

Horizontal Elo intervals are shown for TabPFNv2-compatible datasets (left; 33 datasets, at most 10,000 training rows and 500 features) and TabICL-compatible datasets (right; 36 classification datasets, at most 100,000 rows and 500 features). X-axis is Elo, with ticks from 800 through 1,800. Point/bar endpoints are not numerically printed; do not transcribe pixel-derived Elo values as exact. Text nearby states that TabPFNv2 performs strongly when restricted to its supported subset.

## Figure 5, runtime/improvability, PDF page 8

Left panel plots median inference seconds per 1,000 samples on a logarithmic x-axis against improvability percent. Right panel plots median training time per 1,000 samples against improvability. Ensemble-size trajectory points correspond to 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, and 201 random configurations. The scatter/curve points have no printed per-model values. Nearby prose states approximately 15x and 100x increases in inference time for selected TabM and RealMLP ensemble configurations; preserve these as the authors' prose values rather than readings from the plotted points.
