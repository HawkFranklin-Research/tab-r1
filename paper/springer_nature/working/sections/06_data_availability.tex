\section*{Data availability}
The TCGA and CPTAC data analysed in this study are publicly available from \href{https://www.cbioportal.org}{cBioPortal} (TCGA PanCancer Atlas 2018 studies) and \href{https://www.linkedomics.org}{LinkedOmics}. The 400 frozen patient-grouped test sets, per-patient predictions for every model and test set, and source data for all figures and tables are available in the study repository on \href{https://github.com/HawkFranklin-Research/tab-r1-experiments}{GitHub (HawkFranklin-Research/tab-r1-experiments)}. An archived copy of the frozen test sets, predictions and source data is deposited on figshare (\href{https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34332477}{doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.34332477}).
